# World Flags

A colourful flag quiz. One flag, three country names: one is right, two are wrong. Answer before the
timer runs out.

**Play in a browser:** https://elrity.github.io/streambeam/flags/
**Android APK:** the `world-flags-1.N` releases on the [releases page](https://github.com/elrity/streambeam/releases).
It plays offline; everything is inside the app.

## How to play

1. On the main menu pick a **difficulty level** (1 to 5). Each level has its own flags:
   | Level | Flags | Wrong answers | Default time |
   | --- | --- | --- | --- |
   | 1 Starter | famous flags (USA, Japan, Brazil…) | any country | 10 s |
   | 2 Easy | simple flags (stripes, a circle) | sometimes same continent | 8 s |
   | 3 Medium | more detailed flags | same continent | 6 s |
   | 4 Hard | detailed flags | look-alikes: same colours | 4 s |
   | 5 Expert | the most complex flags (coats of arms, seals) | look-alikes: same colours | 2 s |
2. Drag the **time slider** to choose 10, 8, 6, 4 or 2 seconds per flag (picking a level sets its default).
3. Tap **Play**. Each game is 20 flags and 3 hearts. A wrong answer or running out of time costs a heart.
   After each answer the flag flies off to the left and the next one slides in.

## Score

Each correct answer scores `(100 + up to 150 for speed) × streak × level × timer`:

- **Speed:** answering instantly gives the full 150 bonus, at the buzzer nothing.
- **Streak:** every correct answer in a row adds ×0.25 (up to ×3). A wrong answer resets it.
- **Level:** ×1, ×1.25, ×1.5, ×1.75, ×2 for levels 1 to 5.
- **Timer:** ×1 at 10 s, ×1.2 at 8 s, ×1.45 at 6 s, ×1.75 at 4 s, ×2.2 at 2 s.
- **Perfect game** (20 of 20): a bonus of 1000 × level × timer.

High scores are kept for each level and time.

## Files

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `index.html`, `style.css`, `game.js` | The game. |
| `audio.js` | Background music and sound effects, made live with Web Audio (no sound files). |
| `data.js`, `img/` | 197 country flags with their level. Generated by `tools/build-flags.mjs` from [flag-icons](https://github.com/lipis/flag-icons) (MIT, see `img/LICENSE.txt`). |

The Android app is in [`flags-android/`](../../flags-android): it packs this folder into the APK.
To build it yourself: `cd flags-android && ./gradlew assembleRelease` (needs the Android SDK).
